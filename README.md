# Data Analytics Portfolio

A collection of projects showcasing data analysis, process optimization,
automation, and practical business problem-solving.

## About my work

As a data analyst in the automotive industry, I extracted and transformed
customer data, organized customer statuses, and reconciled sales information
to support sales-representative commission payments. My work involved Python,
pandas, spreadsheets, and SQL-based data sources.

## Projects

| Project | Focus | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Customer Status and Sales Reconciliation](projects/customer-sales-analytics/README.md) | Customer status, sales, refunds, and representative-level reporting | Data cleaning, reconciliation, reporting |
| [Lead Preparation and Funding Analysis](projects/lead-funding-analytics/README.md) | CRM-ready lead data, status tracking, and sales and funding review | ETL, field mapping, data quality |

Each project includes a small **fictional** dataset and an illustrative
analysis. The examples demonstrate a workflow, not real company results or
commission policies. Original customer records, financial reports,
credentials, and company source code are not included.

The customer-sales case study also includes an independently created
[executable ETL demo](projects/customer-sales-analytics/README.md#executable-etl-demonstration)
with generated fictional inputs, exception checks, and an Excel report.
